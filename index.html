NeuroResearch
Self-Correcting AI Research System

A production-grade multi-agent research pipeline where three specialised LLM agents — Research, Writer, and Critique — collaborate in a self-correcting loop. Iterates until the critique score reaches 0.95, performs live DuckDuckGo web searches on every pass, and streams each agent's output in real time via SSE. Ships with a built-in observability dashboard, a 25-topic eval harness, PDF export, and JWT auth.
